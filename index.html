Research areas: Design Intelligence, Creative Learning and Synthetic Cognition.

My work explores the integration of data-driven methods in creative learning and design practices, with a focus on synthetic intelligence.

I investigate how data-driven frameworks can evaluate creativity in architecture and adjacent fields, and how artificial intelligence is reshaping both the learning process and the built environments it influences.

Three research threads

Design × learning × cognition

  • Design Intelligence

    Data-driven frameworks for evaluating creativity in architecture and the fields next to it.

  • Creative Learning

    How generative AI changes the way designers learn, and what design literacy means in the GenAI era.

  • Synthetic Cognition

    Synthetic intelligence as a participant in design practice, and its effect on the built environment.
